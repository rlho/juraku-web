About Us

Tokyo-inspired comfort food with NYC energy. A place where people feel like they belong.

"I wanted to create a place where people feel like they belong."

Founded by

Kiyo

"I think maybe... it's because I've felt broken for a long time."

Izakaya Juraku was built around a simple but powerful idea — everyone deserves a place that feels like home. Kiyo opened Juraku to serve dishes that bring peace and drinks that quiet the mind, creating a genuine community space in the heart of the Lower East Side.

More than a restaurant, Juraku is a sanctuary. A place to decompress, connect, and eat something that actually hits right.

Japanese craft beers. Original cocktails. Premium sakes. An izakaya menu built around comfort — karaage, yakitori, handrolls, Japanese chicken wings.

We serve the kind of food and drinks that make you feel less alone. That's been true since day one, and it'll be true on year ten.
