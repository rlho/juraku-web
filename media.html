If you're a food & beverage content creator, or you run a page focused on Japanese or Asian culture, we'd love to hear from you. While we prioritize NYC-based accounts, we're open to others if the fit feels right.

What We Offer

We provide a complimentary dining experience for two (you and a guest), in exchange for:

  • A collaborative post (tagged + co-posted if possible)
  • Instagram story content from your visit
  • Any additional photos or videos you'd be willing to share afterward (optional, but appreciated!)

When to Visit

To give you the best possible experience, we ask that you avoid Fridays and Saturdays, especially during peak dinner hours. Weekends are our busiest time, and we can't always guarantee the space or attention needed for quality content creation.

We're closed on Mondays.

How to Apply

DM us on Instagram @izakayajuraku or email us at hello@izakayajuraku.com with links to your social pages and a short intro. We'll follow up if we're a good fit.
